# Three model papers to read (for writing and framing)

Chosen because each does, in a different field, what your paper does: show that a *reporting or evaluation choice*
changes which method looks best. Read them as a writer, not only for content. All three are also citations your
Related Work needs (after a full read).

| # | Paper | Why this one | Read for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Steegen, Tuerlinckx, Gelman, Vanpaemel (2016). *Increasing Transparency Through a Multiverse Analysis.* Perspectives on Psychological Science 11(5). doi:10.1177/1745691616658637 | The origin of "multiverse analysis", the method your Study 1 uses | How to **define the set of reasonable choices** and present results across all of them; how to argue that one analysis can mislead without accusing anyone |
| 2 | Dodge, Gururangan, Card, Schwartz, Smith (2019). *Show Your Work: Improved Reporting of Experimental Results.* EMNLP 2019. aclanthology.org/D19-1224 | Closest in spirit: an NLP paper showing that a reporting protocol (compute budget) flips model comparisons, plus a reporting recommendation | **Structure** (problem → method → "conclusions would change" evidence → checklist); how to write a **recommendation section** reviewers accept |
| 3 | Kamalloo, Dziri, Clarke, Rafiei (2023). *Evaluating Open-Domain QA in the Era of LLMs.* ACL 2023. aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.307 | An evaluation-methodology paper in QA, your area; also explains a limit of our Tier 2 F1 metric | How to present an **evaluation artefact with careful human checking**; how to scope claims; how to discuss metric limitations honestly |

## How to read each (about 2 hours per paper)
1. Read the abstract and introduction; write down in 3 sentences: the problem, the gap, the claim.
2. Look only at figures and tables; guess the result; then read the results section.
3. Note 3 sentences or paragraph moves you would like to reuse **as structure** (never copy wording).
4. Write a 5-line note in `literature/notes/<key>.md`: claim, evidence, method, limitation, how it relates to your paper.
   Once you have read the full text, set `verification: L3` in `bibliography.yaml` (then it is citable).

Sources checked 2026-10-19: arXiv records 1909.03004 and 2305.06984 (abstracts), ACL Anthology pages; Steegen et al.
via the journal listing in a search index (L1 until you read the PDF).
